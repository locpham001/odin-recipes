Fried Rice

Description

A plate of Fried Rice made from cold white rice and eggs and some add ons like green onions and chicken

Ingredients

  • Cold overnight white rice
  • 2 eggs
  • Green Onions
  • 200g of shredded chicken

Steps

  1. Preheat pan
  2. Pour oil into pan
  3. Fry egg
  4. Put rice in
  5. Put chicken in
  6. Finish cooking
  7. Garnish with green onion
